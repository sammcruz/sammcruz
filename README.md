## About me

I lead a data engineering team at [isolutions](https://www.isolutions.ch), designing end-to-end data platforms on **Microsoft Fabric**, **Databricks** and **Azure**: ingestion, Spark transformation, medallion lakehouses, semantic models, governance, and the CI/CD and infrastructure-as-code around them.

8+ years in data, across consumer tech (Glovo), global manufacturing (AB InBev), operations (APCOA) and research (NOAA, UFRJ). I started as an **oceanographic engineer** working with satellite and ocean data, then moved into analytics, BI and data engineering. I still like large, messy, real-world data.

I also teach (Databricks instructor since 2024), speak at community events and write about what I learn.
